# Flexibility

Mixing activities helps to keep the body flexible as well as covering more muscle groups in the body.

Yoga and Pilates are specific kinds of activities that promote a flexible body.

Yoga also focuses on breathing technique which allows the muscles of the body to relax and become more flexible (including the brain).
