# Plant Based Diet

Meat and fish are the most expensive food source for humans. The production of meat consumes large amounts of water and other human edible food sources (e.g. 95% of soya bean production is for animal stock).


Simple recipes and cooking tips for a healthy vegetarian and vegan approach to food.


Feel free to raise issues with your own favorite recipes.
