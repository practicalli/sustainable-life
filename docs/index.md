# Practicalli Sustainable Life

Making small and simple changes to your life over time will bring improvements in your well-being, life life more cost effectively and help make the world just a little better.

Living healthily allows your to make the most out of life, feeling better both physically and mentally.

When you can also live sustainably you are helping keep the planet viable for human life.

## Vegetarian and Vegan diet

Adopting a [Plant-based Diet](./introduction/plant-diet.md) is a simple way to improve your own health whist making a significant difference for the rest of humanity.

Meat and fish are the most expensive food source for humans.

The production of meat consumes large amounts of water and other human edible food sources (e.g. 95% of soya bean production is for animal stock).

Industrial fishing causes significant damage to the oceans and floor beds. Coupled with widespread pollution, humans are placing oceanic life at far greater risk.
